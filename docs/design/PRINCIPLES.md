# Principles

1. **Design is source of truth** — Stitch/Figma/specs override improvisation.
2. **Cinematic first viewport** — brand, one headline, one line, CTAs, full-bleed media.
3. **Editorial over dashboard** — asymmetric grids, storytelling sections.
4. **Animation communicates** — hierarchy, space, progress — never decoration alone.
5. **CMS-first** — content changes without code.
6. **Security by default** — no secrets or raw CCTV URLs in the client.
7. **States are product** — loading / empty / error / offline are designed, not afterthoughts.
8. **Responsive compositions** — redesign for breakpoints; do not shrink.
9. **Trust through transparency** — progress, live sites, and craftsmanship are visible and controlled.
10. **Restraint** — bronze accent sparingly; whitespace and imagery do the work.
